We Built This
For You.

UNDRGRND Docs was born out of frustration. Frustration with mainstream platforms that demonetize raw true crime, age-restrict bodycam footage, and shadowban independent street journalists just for documenting reality.

If you cover intense topics—like crime, raw police encounters, or unfiltered historical events—the corporate gatekeepers have decided your stories are "unsafe for advertisers." You shouldn't have to censor your own documentaries just to survive the algorithm.

The "Upload to Underground First" Program

We created this platform specifically to rescue channels that cover the darkest and most intense aspects of human reality. You don't need a million subscribers to make a living here. Our main goal is to support and fund creators like you.

The Subscription Model

UNDRGRND Docs is entirely subscriber-funded. Users pay a flat monthly subscription to access the unvarnished truth. Because we answer to our subscribers and not to corporate advertisers, there is zero censorship and zero demonetization.

The Payout Engine

We pay out based on view time. 100% of the platform's revenue pool is distributed directly to creators based on how long users watch your documentaries.

  • Upload First Exclusivity: This is our core promise. Creators who sign to upload their raw, uncensored cuts to UNDRGRND first (before putting a censored version on YouTube) receive a massive 1.5x multiplier on their view-time payouts.
  • Fair Scaling: Smaller, emerging creators get specialized multipliers to ensure they earn a sustainable living wage for their early work, while established channels earn premium rates for driving massive subscriber volume.

You provide the raw stories. We provide the uncensored infrastructure. You get paid. Welcome to the Underground.

Legal & Policies

Clear, transparent, and uncensorious rules of engagement.

1. Terms of Service & Subscription Terms

By subscribing to UNDRGRND Docs, users agree to pay a recurring monthly fee for unrestricted access to the platform's content. Subscriptions can be canceled at any time. We do not offer refunds for partial billing cycles. Users agree not to rip, pirate, or redistribute exclusive content hosted on this platform.

2. Creator Agreement & Payouts

Creators retain 100% ownership of their intellectual property. UNDRGRND merely licenses the right to stream the content. Payouts are calculated dynamically at the end of each month based on the creator's share of total platform view time. Exclusivity agreements are opt-in and require the creator to refrain from posting the licensed documentary on competing video platforms for a period of 12 months.

3. Content Policy (Uncensored, but Legal)

We do not censor based on political ideology, harsh realities, or advertiser preferences. However, we are a legal entity. We strictly prohibit: (a) Content depicting unsimulated, non-consensual violence or abuse; (b) CSAM; (c) Direct incitement to imminent lawless action. Raw street journalism, combat footage, and controversial political documentaries are expressly protected.

4. Privacy Policy

We collect minimal data. We track view time exclusively to calculate creator payouts. We do not sell user data to data brokers, advertising agencies, or third-party tracking networks. Your billing information is handled securely by Stripe.

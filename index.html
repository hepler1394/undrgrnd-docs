We Built This
For You.

UNDRGRND Docs was born from a simple problem: independent reporting and difficult documentary subjects do not always fit the incentives of large, advertiser-supported platforms.

If you cover intense topics—crime, raw police encounters or unfiltered historical events—you deserve a distribution conversation based on the work, the rights and the audience, not only its advertising category.

The First Release Program

We created this platform to give emerging documentary makers and field reporters a direct relationship with viewers. Optional first-window releases are negotiated project by project; creators never give up ownership merely by applying.

The Subscription Model

UNDRGRND Docs is being designed around subscriber support rather than behavioral advertising. Subscription pricing, launch timing and final creator terms will be published before paid access begins.

The Payout Engine

The proposed model assigns 75% of net subscription revenue to a creator pool allocated by eligible view time. Net revenue means money remaining after payment processing, refunds and required taxes.

  • Optional first window: Additional weighting may be offered for a defined first-release period, but only when a creator agreement states the duration and calculation.
  • No guaranteed earnings: Audience demand and the total creator pool change each month. Worked examples are labeled and are not promises of income.

You provide the reporting. We provide distribution tools, a direct viewer relationship and clearly documented terms. Any payment starts only after both sides sign a creator agreement.

Legal & Policies

Plain-language launch policies. These are not a substitute for a final creator agreement or the full notices required before paid subscriptions begin.

1. Current viewer access

UNDRGRND Docs is in a free pre-launch stage. An account is required to watch, but no paid subscription is currently offered and this page does not authorize a recurring charge. Pricing, cancellation, refund and payment-provider terms will be shown for affirmative acceptance before billing begins. Users may not copy or redistribute films beyond the license shown for each title.

2. Creator Agreement & Payouts

Creators retain ownership of their intellectual property. UNDRGRND streams creator-supplied work only under a written license. The proposed launch model allocates a 75% share of net subscription revenue to a creator pool based on eligible watch time. Any first-window or exclusivity term is optional, project-specific and valid only when written in the signed agreement. Earnings are not guaranteed.

3. Editorial and content policy

Editorial decisions are not based solely on advertiser preference or political viewpoint. Every submission is still reviewed for lawful rights, consent, privacy, safety, accuracy and platform risk. Prohibited material includes child sexual abuse material, credible threats, unlawful incitement, non-consensual intimate imagery, instructions facilitating serious wrongdoing and uploads the creator does not have the right to distribute. Newsworthy context is considered, but publication is never guaranteed.

4. Privacy Policy

We use account information to provide access, secure administrator tools and respond to creator applications. Viewing progress is currently stored in the browser and account records are stored in Firebase. Creator applications are visible to the verified site administrator. We do not sell personal data. A complete privacy notice, retention schedule, deletion request process and payment-provider disclosure will be published before subscriptions begin. Privacy requests can be sent to hello@undrgrnddocs.com.
